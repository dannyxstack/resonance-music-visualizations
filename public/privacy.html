Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-25

Media processed on your device

Selected music, images imported into the page and generated videos are processed locally in your browser. These features do not upload those files to our servers. We do not use those files to train models.

Data we store

When you register, our server stores your email, password hash, account creation time, sessions, plan status, export records, favorites and scene configurations you choose to save. Configurations can include titles, author names and other text you enter; avoid sensitive information. Passwords are not stored as plaintext.

Browser storage holds language choices, favorites and some scene preferences. Sign-in uses a session cookie. Hosting services may generate necessary operational logs such as request paths, IP addresses and errors.

Purposes and service providers

We use this information for sign-in, configuration storage, favorite syncing, export limits, support and service protection. Hosting, database and email providers process data as needed to provide these services; processing may occur outside Australia.

Payments are not open and this site does not collect card details. When external checkout is enabled, we will identify the payment provider and update the relevant data processing information.

Retention, deletion and contact

Accounts and configurations are retained as needed to provide the service. Delete saved configurations from the scene panel; contact us to request account deletion or access to your personal data. Transaction, security or dispute records may be retained longer where required by law. Local storage can be cleared in browser settings.
