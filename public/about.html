About BeatLuma

Let the beat become light

BeatLuma is a browser-based music visualization tool by Novakite Studio. Choose local music, bring cities, the Earth, neon and cosmic roads to life with sound, and export video with your audio.

No desktop installation is required. A desktop browser is recommended for creation; audio analysis, visuals and video encoding depend on your device and browser.
