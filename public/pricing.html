Before you start

Prices are in US dollars. When purchasing opens, applicable taxes and the final total will be shown before payment confirmation. We do not take paid pre-orders or collect card details at this stage.

Videos render in your browser. Practical duration, resolution and export success depend on your device, memory, codecs and browser support. Pro does not include cloud rendering.

Most scenes export WebM in real time, taking approximately the clip duration. Space Odyssey can use frame-by-frame export and prefer MP4 in supported browsers. MP4 is not available on every device or scene.

Cloud configurations save settings and text, not music or imported images. Reopening a configuration or changing devices requires selecting local media again. Failed or canceled exports do not use Free credits; an export counts after successful encoding and download initiation.

Questions

Is my music uploaded?

Your selected music, images and generated video are processed locally in the browser. Accounts, favorites and configurations you choose to save are stored on the server.

Can I publish or use the output commercially?

You may publish and commercially use exports to the extent you hold the necessary rights. A BeatLuma plan does not license third-party music, trademarks, likenesses or images. Check your chosen media and any third-party content in a scene.

Can I buy Pro now?

Not yet. The working product is available to try for free. Pro purchasing will open after payment onboarding and fulfillment testing are complete.
