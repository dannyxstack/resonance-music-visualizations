Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-25

Paid purchasing is not open. The following rules apply to the Pro monthly plan once purchasing becomes available.

Refund requests

Within 14 days of your first Pro purchase, you may contact support to request a full refund of the initial payment. Include your account email, order ID and a short explanation. Contact us promptly about duplicate charges, access not being activated or other payment errors.

Renewal and cancellation

Pro renews monthly. Once purchasing is open, cancel the next renewal through the provided subscription management route, or contact support if it is unavailable. Access continues until the paid period ends and no further renewal is taken. We generally do not prorate the current period, without affecting statutory rights or remedies for payment errors.

Processing and return of funds

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Arrival time depends on the payment provider and bank. The corresponding paid access ends when a full refund is approved.

Statutory consumer rights

This policy does not limit rights under Australian Consumer Law or other applicable laws. We will provide remedies or refunds required by law even outside the 14-day period.
